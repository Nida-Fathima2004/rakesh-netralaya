Diseases

What is Cataract?

A cataract is a clouding of the natural lens of the eye. It is the most common cause of vision loss in people over age 40 and is the principal cause of blindness in the world. It usually develops slowly and can affect one or both eyes. Modern treatment involves surgical removal.

  • Symptoms: Cloudy/blurry vision, fading of colors, poor night vision.
  • Treatment: Surgical removal and replacement with an artificial intraocular lens (IOL).
Consult a Specialist

What is Glaucoma?

Glaucoma is a group of eye conditions that damage the optic nerve, the health of which is vital for good vision. This damage is often caused by an abnormally high pressure in your eye. It is one of the leading causes of blindness.

  • Symptoms: Often no symptoms initially, leading to silent vision loss. Peripheral vision loss is common.
  • Treatment: Eyedrops, oral medication, laser treatment, or surgery to lower eye pressure.
Consult a Specialist

Diabetic Retinopathy

A diabetes-related eye condition that damages the retina due to prolonged high blood sugar levels. Early detection is crucial.

  • Symptoms: Blurred vision, floaters, vision loss.
  • Treatment: Laser therapy, injections, or surgery.
Consult a Specialist

Squint (Strabismus)

A condition where the eyes are misaligned and do not look in the same direction at the same time.

  • Symptoms: Crossed eyes, double vision.
  • Treatment: Glasses, exercises, or surgery.
Consult a Specialist

Retinal Detachment

A serious condition where the retina pulls away from its normal position, requiring immediate medical attention.

  • Symptoms: Sudden flashes, floaters, shadow over vision.
  • Treatment: Laser or surgical repair.
Consult a Specialist

Macular Hole

A small break in the macula that affects central vision and reading ability.

  • Symptoms: Blurred or distorted central vision.
  • Treatment: Vitrectomy surgery.
Consult a Specialist

Uveitis

Inflammation of the middle layer of the eye that can cause redness, pain, and vision loss.

  • Symptoms: Eye pain, redness, light sensitivity.
  • Treatment: Medication and specialist care.
Consult a Specialist
